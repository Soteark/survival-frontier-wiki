    Scannerbot¶

    The Scannerbot is Survival's flying drone. It patrols the roads at night, scans for players, and hunts them down with homing missiles. It's part of the base game, and Survival Frontier leaves it working as normal, but it adds one thing: the host can switch the Scannerbot off for their world, and back on, with a chat command. Nothing in the story needs the drone, so turning it off doesn't block your progress.

    At a glance

    • A flying missile drone from the base game. It's on by default, just like vanilla.
    • Only comes out at night, in the overworld, and only after a point early in the story.
    • It can't be damaged. Weapons, hammers and explosions don't hurt it.
    • Type /scannerbot to see whether it's on in this world.
    • Host only: /scannerbot off removes it from the world, and /scannerbot on brings it back. The setting is saved with the world.

    What it does¶

    • Night patrols. After dark the Scannerbot flies out along the roads, scanning as it goes. It doesn't come out in the day.
    • Hunting. It keeps track of where players have been. Some nights it goes hunting: it moves in close to a player and attacks with homing missiles.
    • More aggressive at first. Its first few hunts happen whatever you do. After that, it waits more than a full day-night cycle between hunts.
    • Overworld only. It doesn't follow you into other worlds.

    Fighting it¶

    You can't destroy the Scannerbot. Shots and melee hits only spark off it, and explosions do nothing.

    Your options are to avoid it:

    • Stay off the roads at night, since that's where it patrols.
    • Or turn it off for your world (below).

    Turning it on or off¶

    Command Who What it does
    /scannerbot anyone Says whether the Scannerbot is ON or OFF in this world
    /scannerbot off host Turns it off. It won't appear in this world any more, and a drone that's flying right now is removed
    /scannerbot on host Turns it back on. It patrols the roads at night again
    • The setting is saved with the world, so it stays the way you left it after a reload.
    • It's on in every world until the host turns it off.
    • When the host changes it, every player is told who changed it, for example "Alex turned the Scannerbot OFF".
    • If a player who isn't the host tries to change it, they get "Only the host can turn the Scannerbot on or off."
    • When you turn it back on, the base game starts a new drone the next time it would normally appear.

    Tip

    Turning the Scannerbot off doesn't stop the story, quests or other enemies. It only removes the flying drone.
