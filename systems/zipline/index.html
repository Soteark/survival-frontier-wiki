    Zipline¶

    Test build

    The Zipline is still being tested. It's in every world from the start, but how it works may change in later updates.

    String a cable between two Zipline Anchors up to a kilometre apart, hang a Zipline Trolley on it and ride. Wire an Inverter into an anchor and a motor drives you along; without power you slide downhill and pull yourself the rest of the way.

    At a glance

    • Craft: both parts at the Craftbot, known from the start, no blueprint needed.
    • Cable: Use one anchor, then Use another 4 to 1000 m away. Up to 4 cables per anchor.
    • Trolley: Tinker on an anchor hangs one on the cable. Use it to sit, W / S to move, Use or Jump to get off.
    • Speed: 10 m/s on a powered cable. Unpowered: slides downhill up to 7 m/s, or pull yourself along at 2.5 m/s.
    • Power: wire an Inverter into either anchor. One charge runs the motor for 60 seconds of driving.

    Getting the parts¶

    Part Crafted at Ingredients Time Stack
    Zipline Anchor Craftbot 12 × Metal Block, 1 × Component Kit 20 s 10
    Zipline Trolley Craftbot 10 × Metal Block, 2 × Component Kit, 1 × Circuit Board 30 s 5

    The anchor is a tall pole (5 × 11 × 5 blocks). The cable ties on at its tip, so put anchors on top of towers or hills.

    Stringing a cable¶

    1. Press Use on the first anchor: "Cable started".

    2. Press Use on a second anchor to finish it: "Cable strung: N m".

    3. Using the first anchor again cancels.

    4. Using two anchors that already share a cable takes that cable down.
    5. Too far: cables reach at most 1000 m. Too close: anchors need 4 m between them.
    6. An anchor holds at most 4 cables, so one tower can be the hub of several lines.

    Hanging a trolley¶

    Press Tinker on an anchor that has a cable. The trolley goes on the cable closest to where you're looking, and one Zipline Trolley is taken from your inventory (free with unlimited inventory).

    • The trolley and its rider need 3.2 m of clear space under the cable. If there isn't room: "No room under this cable... Put the anchors higher".
    • A trolley placed by hand also hooks on if its pulley ends up close to a cable.

    Riding¶

    Press Use on the trolley to sit in it.

    Cable W / S
    Powered the motor drives you at up to 10 m/s
    Unpowered pull yourself along at 2.5 m/s; on a slope you also slide downhill by yourself, up to 7 m/s
    • It brakes and stops by itself 1.6 m before each anchor, and stops before anything in the way under the cable ahead.
    • An empty trolley holds still on the cable.
    • Use or Jump gets you off.

    Power¶

    Wire an Inverter into either anchor and the whole cable is powered (see Power). Looking at an anchor shows Powered or No power (wire an Inverter in for the motor).

    • One charge from the Inverter runs the motor for 60 seconds. Charge is only used while a trolley is actually being driven by the motor.
    • Power wired in from anything other than an Inverter is cut.

    Calling a trolley¶

    An anchor takes 2 wires in total. Wire a Button, Switch or Sensor into it: each time it turns on, the nearest empty trolley on that anchor's cables comes over to the anchor. A trolley already right beside the anchor isn't called, and a call gives up after a few seconds if something blocks the way.

    Good to know

    • Picking up an anchor removes its cables. A trolley on a removed cable falls.
    • Cables stay strung when nobody is near them.
    • A trolley pushed well off its cable for a few seconds lets go.
