    Frontier Kitchen¶

    The Frontier Kitchen turns the Cookbot into a cooking ladder. There are 35 Frontier dishes spread over five tiers: Classics, Campfire, Homestead, Expedition and Frontier Feast. Each tier heals more than the one below it and gives stronger buffs: health regeneration, damage resistance, deep breath (no running out of air underwater) and random perks. You start with the Classics. Cooking the dishes you know earns mastery, and you spend mastery to research the next tier's dishes at the Cookbot. Before you have a Cookbot, a Campfire built from scrap can cook the simple Classics. Some dishes can't be researched at all. The Traveling Trader sells those recipes, and they heal a little more and their buffs last longer.

    At a glance

    • Five tiers: Classics, then Campfire, Homestead, Expedition and Frontier Feast. All dishes in a tier have the same stats. Only the ingredients differ.
    • Buffs: Regeneration (all tiers), Damage resistance (Homestead and up), Deep breath (Expedition and up), random perks (Expedition gives 1, Frontier Feast gives 2).
    • Research: collecting a cooked dish gives +1 mastery in its tier. Researching a dish costs 3 / 5 / 8 / 12 mastery from the tier below.
    • Shared by the party: mastery and researched recipes belong to the whole world. Buffs belong to each player.
    • Buffs don't stack. Eating again only replaces a buff that is weaker, or equally strong with less time left.
    • Campfire: crafted at the Workbench from 12 Scrap Wood and 4 Scrap Metal. It cooks the three Frontier Classics, one at a time.
    • /food shows your party's mastery, which dishes are researched and your active buffs.

    The five tiers¶

    All dishes in a tier are equally strong. The table shows a dish you researched (or a Classic). Trader dishes are covered below.

    Tier Heals Regeneration Damage resistance Deep breath Perks
    Classics 48 HP +1 HP/s for 15 s – – –
    Campfire 60 HP +1 HP/s for 30 s – – –
    Homestead 80 HP +2 HP/s for 40 s 15% for 3 min – –
    Expedition 92 HP +3 HP/s for 45 s 25% for 5 min 3 min 1 random perk
    Frontier Feast 98 HP +4 HP/s for 75 s 35% for 8 min 5 min 2 random perks

    Here is how much health regeneration gives back over its full duration, if you are hurt the whole time: Classics 15, Campfire 30, Homestead 80, Expedition 135, Frontier Feast 300.

    What the buffs do¶

    • Regeneration heals the listed amount once per second while you are conscious and below full health.
    • Damage resistance reduces all damage you take by the listed percentage.
    • Deep breath keeps your air full for its whole duration, so you can stay underwater without running out of breath.
    • Perks are the game's normal food perks. Each one is picked at random from those you don't have yet: Bonus Health (+25 max HP), Hammer Speed, Fall Protection and High Jump. When you already have all four, more perks do nothing.

    Knocked out = buffs gone

    Being knocked out removes all your food buffs and perks, including the +25 max HP from Bonus Health. A dish eaten while you are knocked out gives no buffs.

    Health is capped at 100¶

    Your maximum health is 100 HP, or 125 with the Bonus Health perk. Healing past your maximum is wasted. So above Homestead, the big jump between tiers is in the buffs, not the heal itself. The trader's Frontier Feast dishes heal 103, and the extra 3 only counts if Bonus Health has raised your maximum.

    Dishes by tier¶

    Ingredients, cook times and which dishes are trader recipes are listed on the Dishes page.

    Tier Researchable / always known Traveling Trader recipes
    Classics Baked Potato, Corn on the Cob, Grilled Fish, plus the game's own Pizza Burger, Veggie Burger and Revival Baguette –
    Campfire Tomato Soup, Corn Chowder, Mashed Potatoes, Carrot Soup, Cornbread, Milk Tea Trail Hash, Fish & Chips
    Homestead Steak & Mash, Berry Pancakes, Banana Bread, Root Stew, Pot Roast, Berry Banana Shake Shepherd's Pie, Borscht, Berry Cobbler
    Expedition Five-Alarm Chili, Tropical Smoothie, Broccoli Gratin, Fish Chowder, Smoked Pike Moonfin Curry, Smoked Eel Rolls, Orange-Glazed Steak
    Frontier Feast Ember-Seared Steak, Glow Risotto, Crystal Parfait, Sturgeon Feast Leviathan Steak, Ember Tarts, Crystal Sorbet

    Campfire-tier ingredients all grow or turn up around the crash site. Every dish from Homestead up needs at least one ingredient that you only find farther out, so the kitchen keeps pulling you into new areas. Several dishes use fish or Fish Fillets from Fishing.

    The game's own Classics

    Pizza Burger, Veggie Burger and Revival Baguette sit in the Classics row and earn Classics mastery when cooked. They keep their normal effects, though, and give no Frontier buffs. The Frontier Classics (Baked Potato, Corn on the Cob, Grilled Fish) are the ones that give 48 HP and Regeneration.

    Check the raid cost

    Each dish's description in your inventory says what raid level you would trigger by planting the farmed crops for one cook. Use it to judge how big a farm a recipe really needs.

    Research and mastery¶

    Cooking is how the kitchen levels up:

    1. Earn mastery. Each time you collect a finished dish from a Cookbot or Campfire, you get +1 mastery in that dish's tier. Mastery is counted per cook, not per item. A Campfire-tier recipe makes 2 dishes but still gives 1 mastery. If you collect several queued cooks at once, you get mastery for each of them.
    2. Spend it to research. Select a locked (dimmed) dish in the Cookbot window. Its description shows the research cost and how much mastery you have. The button reads Research (cost) and works once the tier below has enough mastery. Researching spends that mastery.
    3. Cook the new dish to earn mastery in its own tier, and so on up the ladder.

    Each tier's cost is paid in mastery from the tier directly below it:

    To research a... Costs Paid with Researchable dishes Cooks to research the whole tier
    Campfire dish 3 Classics mastery 6 18 Classics cooks
    Homestead dish 5 Campfire mastery 6 30 Campfire cooks
    Expedition dish 8 Homestead mastery 5 40 Homestead cooks
    Frontier Feast dish 12 Expedition mastery 4 48 Expedition cooks

    Classics are always known and are never researched. Any dish in a tier counts toward that tier's mastery, so you can cook the cheapest recipe over and over.

    Party-wide

    Mastery and researched recipes are saved with the world and shared by everyone in it. Anyone can cook to add mastery, and anyone can spend it. When a dish is researched, everyone gets a chat message. Food buffs are personal: each player has their own.

    Trader dishes¶

    Eleven dishes can't be researched. In the Cookbot they show as Traveling Trader recipes, and the button reads "At the Trader". Their recipes are sold on the Recipes tab of the Traveling Trader's Stall for Frontier Scrip. A tier's trader recipes go on sale once the party has researched at least one regular dish of that tier. After you buy one, the whole party can cook it at any Cookbot like any other dish, and cooking it earns mastery for its tier.

    Trader dishes are a step up from the regular dishes in their tier:

    • Extra health: +5 HP over the tier's normal heal.
    • Buffs last 1.2x as long (rounded to the nearest second). Buff strength and perks are the same as the tier's.
    Tier Recipe price Heals Regeneration Damage resistance Deep breath Perks
    Campfire 36 scrip 65 HP +1 HP/s for 36 s – – –
    Homestead 72 scrip 85 HP +2 HP/s for 48 s 15% for 3 min 36 s – –
    Expedition 80 scrip 97 HP +3 HP/s for 54 s 25% for 6 min 3 min 36 s 1 random perk
    Frontier Feast 192 scrip 103 HP +4 HP/s for 90 s 35% for 9 min 36 s 6 min 2 random perks

    When you eat a trader dish, the message on screen calls it a "trader's special" instead of a "meal".

    The Cookbot window¶

    The mod replaces the game's three-button Cookbot window with a grid of every dish. The Classics are on the first row, then one row per tier. Each row's label shows the tier and the party's current mastery in it, for example "Homestead - 7 mastery". Dishes you know are shown in full colour, and locked dishes are dimmed.

    Selecting a dish shows its name, a 3D preview, its description, its ingredients and its cook time. The button changes with the dish:

    Dish state Button
    Known, and you have the ingredients Craft
    Known, but ingredients are missing Craft (greyed out)
    Locked, researchable Research (3 / 5 / 8 / 12), active once you have enough mastery in the tier below
    Locked Traveling Trader recipe At the Trader (greyed out)

    All dishes / Cookable now¶

    Two tabs at the top filter the grid:

    • All dishes shows every dish.
    • Cookable now shows only dishes you know and have every ingredient for, right now. Ingredients are counted from your own inventory plus every chest piped into the Cookbot, the same places the Cookbot takes them from when it cooks. The list updates about four times a second while the window is open, so it follows along as you fill chests or your inventory. In Creative mode (unlimited inventory), every known dish counts as cookable.

    The tab you pick stays selected for every Cookbot until you leave the game.

    Cook times¶

    The Cookbot cooks one order at a time.

    Tier Cook time Dishes per cook
    Classics (Frontier) 10 s 1
    Campfire 20 s 2
    Homestead 30 s 1
    Expedition 45 s 1
    Frontier Feast 60 s 1

    The Campfire¶

    The Campfire is how you start cooking before you have a Cookbot. Its recipe is known from the start.
    Crafted at Workbench (in your crashed ship)
    Recipe 12 Scrap Wood + 4 Scrap Metal, 10 s
    Cooks The three Frontier Classics only: Baked Potato, Corn on the Cob, Grilled Fish
    Cook time 30 s per dish, one dish at a time
    Earns +1 Classics mastery per dish collected

    To use it, press Use on the Campfire, pick a dish and press Craft. The fire burns while the dish cooks. When it's done, collect the dish from the process slot. If your inventory is full the dish stays on the fire and you get a warning.

    Compared to the Cookbot, the Campfire:

    • Cooks only the Frontier Classics. It can't cook the game's own Classics or any higher tier.
    • Takes 30 s per dish instead of 10 s.
    • Uses only ingredients from your inventory, not from chests piped into it.
    • Has no research and no Cookable now tab.

    Bank mastery early

    Classics mastery earned at the Campfire is saved. Cook there while you work toward a Cookbot, and you can research your first Campfire-tier dishes as soon as it's built. At 3 mastery per dish, 18 Classics cooks research all six.

    Buff stacking and refresh¶

    Food buffs don't stack. Each buff type (Regeneration, Damage resistance, Deep breath) is tracked on its own. When you eat, each buff the dish gives:

    • replaces your current buff of that type if the new one is stronger, or
    • replaces it if it is equally strong and lasts longer than the time you have left on the current one,
    • and is ignored otherwise.

    What this means in practice:

    • Eating the same tier again refreshes your buffs to full duration.
    • A lower tier never overwrites a higher one. A Homestead dish won't cut short a Frontier Feast's 35% resistance.
    • A stronger buff always wins, even if it is shorter. Expedition's +3 HP/s for 45 s replaces Homestead's +2 HP/s however much time Homestead had left.
    • Expedition and Frontier Feast give the same Deep breath, so the longer one wins. A trader dish's longer buffs also win at equal strength.
    • Perks are always granted, whatever happened to the buffs.
    • If nothing changed, the on-screen message says "already well fed". You still get the health.

    Buffs are saved with your character and keep counting down while you're in the game. When a buff runs out, a chat message tells you.

    HUD buff icons¶

    Each active food buff shows as an icon in the status panel at the bottom left of the screen, after the game's perk icons. The order is always Regeneration, Damage resistance, Deep breath. Hover over an icon to see its strength and time left, for example "Damage resistance 25% - 4 min 12 s left". An icon shines once when you gain the buff or top it up, just like a new perk does, and it disappears when the buff runs out.

    When you eat a Frontier dish, a message shows what you gained, for example "Expedition meal: Regeneration +3/s, Damage resistance 25%, Deep breath, 1 perk".

    The /food command¶

    Type /food in chat for a full kitchen report:

    1. Cooking mastery: the party's current mastery in each tier.
    2. Every Frontier dish and its status: known, research for N mastery, or sold by the Traveling Trader for N scrip.
    3. Your food buffs: each active buff with its time left, or "No food buffs active".

    See Commands for the other chat commands.

    Dishes elsewhere¶

    • Contracts: Supply Runs can ask for dishes of a tier, and contract rewards often include a few dishes.
    • Fishing: Fish Fillets and whole fish go into Grilled Fish, Fish Chowder, Smoked Pike, Sturgeon Feast and several trader dishes.
