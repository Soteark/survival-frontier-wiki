    Remote Control¶

    Drive and work your creations without sitting in them. A Remote Controller is a driver seat nobody sits in: build it into a creation, aim a handheld Remote at it and you steer with WASD and work its parts with the hotbar from outside. Remote Cameras let you watch from the creation itself, and a Remote Station lets you drive from any empty chair. The same family of parts includes Wireless Transmitters and Receivers (logic signals with no wire between them) and Teleport Pads (jump between pads on the same channel, anywhere in the world).

    At a glance

    • Remote Controller: 5 levels, 6 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 20 connections, upgraded with Component Kits like a Driver Seat.
    • Remote: aim within 7.5 m and press Create to take control. Right-click reconnects to your last controller from any distance.
    • Kept loaded: while you drive, the 3 x 3 map cells around the controller (192 x 192 m) stay loaded, and for 10 seconds after you let go.
    • Remote Cameras: up to 4 per controller. Jump cycles through them.
    • Wireless: 20 channels. Both ends have to be in a loaded part of the world.
    • Teleport Pads: 20 channels, any number of pads per channel. Each jump costs 1 charge from a wired Inverter, or 1 Battery from your inventory.
    • How to get them: all are Craftbot recipes unlocked by Traveling Trader blueprints.

    How to get them¶

    Every part on this page is built in the Craftbot, but only after the party owns its blueprint from the Traveling Trader. A blueprint unlocks its recipes for the whole party. Rarer blueprints need more trader reputation (see the trader page).

    Item Blueprint Rarity Price Needs first
    Remote Controller (level 1) + Remote Remote Control Rare 144 scrip -
    Remote Camera Remote Camera Rare 144 scrip Remote Control
    Wireless Transmitter + Receiver Wireless Link Uncommon 72 scrip -
    Teleport Pad Teleport Pad Legendary 300 scrip -

    Craftbot recipes:

    Item Ingredients Craft time
    Remote Controller 1 Circuit Board, 2 Component Kits, 4 Metal Blocks 20 s
    Remote 2 Circuit Boards, 1 Battery, 2 Component Kits 20 s
    Remote Camera 1 Circuit Board, 1 Component Kit, 2 Metal Blocks 15 s
    Wireless Transmitter 1 Circuit Board, 1 Component Kit, 1 Battery 15 s
    Wireless Receiver 1 Circuit Board, 1 Component Kit, 2 Metal Blocks 15 s
    Teleport Pad 4 Circuit Boards, 3 Batteries, 8 Component Kits, 20 Metal Blocks 60 s

    Only level 1 of the Remote Controller is crafted. Higher levels come from upgrading it (below). See also Recipes and Items.

    Remote Controller¶

    Wire it exactly like a Driver Seat: engines, steering bearings, pistons and hotbar parts (thrusters, drills, switches and so on). Nobody can sit in it. Instead, a player with a Remote takes control of it and their input drives the creation.

    Press Tinker on it to open the upgrade window. Upgrades cost Component Kits and follow the same steps as the vanilla Driver Seat:

    Level Connections Cost to reach this level Extra
    1 6 (crafted)
    2 8 1 Component Kit
    3 12 2 Component Kits
    4 16 3 Component Kits
    5 20 5 Component Kits Steering joint settings can be adjusted, like a level 5 Driver Seat

    Going from level 1 to level 5 costs 11 Component Kits in total.

    • One pilot at a time. The first player to take control drives it. Nobody else's steering counts until that player lets go.
    • The area stays loaded. While someone is driving, the controller keeps the 3 x 3 map cells around it loaded (each cell is 64 m, so 192 x 192 m), moving with the creation. It holds them for another 10 seconds after the pilot lets go, so the creation doesn't freeze mid-move.
    • Up to 4 Remote Cameras can be wired into it. They don't take any of its connections.

    Tip

    Upgrading keeps your pairing. Right-click reconnect still finds an upgraded controller in the same spot.

    Remote¶

    The handheld that drives a Remote Controller. Equip it from the hotbar.

    Control What it does
    Aim at a controller (within 7.5 m) + Create Take control. This also pairs the controller with you.
    W A S D Drive and steer, like sitting in the seat
    Sprint Sprint, as in a seat
    1 - 0 Press the controller's hotbar slots
    Create / Attack Also press hotbar slots 1 and 2, as in a normal seat
    Jump Cycle through the controller's Remote Cameras and back to your own view
    Use Let go. Putting the Remote away also lets go.
    Attack (right-click) while not aiming at a controller Reconnect to your paired controller, from any distance

    Your character stands still while you drive.

    Reconnecting from far away¶

    The game remembers the last controller each player took control of, and that pairing is saved with the world. Right-click with the Remote (not aimed at a controller) to reconnect:

    • If the controller is loaded, you connect straight away.
    • If it isn't, you see "Remote: waking up controller..." and the area around its last known spot is loaded. When it shows up you're connected. It gets 15 seconds to show up.
    • If it doesn't show up in time, or it was picked up or destroyed, you get "Remote: controller is gone" and the pairing is cleared. Take control of a controller again to pair a new one.
    • With no controller paired yet you get "Remote: no controller paired yet".

    Warning

    The last known spot is updated while the controller is being driven and whenever it is loaded. A creation that was moved some other way while you weren't around may not be where the Remote expects it.

    Remote Station¶

    Sit in any vanilla seat (Seat, Saddle, Driver Seat, Driver Saddle or Gyro Seat) that has nothing wired to it: no hotbar parts and no bearings or pistons. If you have a Remote anywhere in your inventory you see "Remote Station - press Create to take control of your Remote Controller".

    • Press Create (or 1) to take control of your paired controller. If it isn't loaded, it is woken up the same way as the Remote's reconnect.
    • You don't need the Remote in your hand, only in your inventory.
    • The seat's hotbar is hidden and the controller's is shown while you drive. WASD, the hotbar, Jump for cameras and Use all work as with the handheld Remote.
    • Use lets go of the controller and keeps you seated. Press Use again to stand up.
    • Taking control with the Remote while you're sitting (aiming it, or right-click reconnect) also drives from the seat.

    Tip

    A seat that has anything wired to it works as a normal seat, and Create is left to it. Use a spare, unwired chair as your station.

    Remote Cameras¶

    A camera for remote-controlled creations.

    • Wiring: wire from the camera into the Remote Controller (camera first, then the controller). Each camera has one output; a controller takes up to 4 cameras.
    • Facing: the camera looks out of its front face, the same way a mounted gun fires.
    • Switching views: while driving, press Jump. The view cycles Camera 1, Camera 2, ... and then back to your own view ("Your view"). With no camera wired, Jump tells you "No Remote Camera wired to this controller".
    • Letting go of the controller always returns you to your own view.

    Wireless Transmitter and Receiver¶

    A logic signal with no wire in between. Transmitters and receivers pair by channel: there is no linking step. Set both to the same channel and they're connected.

    Transmitter Receiver
    Wire into it Up to 10 logic inputs: buttons, switches, logic gates, sensors, seat hotbar slots Nothing
    Wire out of it Nothing Up to 255 outputs: engines, doors, lights, logic, anything a switch can drive
    On when Any part wired into it is on Any Transmitter on its channel is on
    • Channels: 1 to 20. New parts start on channel 1. Use goes to the next channel and Tinker to the previous one, wrapping around (20 goes to 1). Look at the part to see its channel.
    • Several transmitters on one channel act like an OR gate: the receivers are on while any of them is on.
    • Range: no distance limit, but both ends have to be in a loaded part of the world, near a player. A transmitter in an unloaded area counts as off.
    • Channels are shared by everyone in the world. In multiplayer, agree on channels so you don't drive each other's receivers.

    Teleport Pads¶

    Pads on the same channel are linked, wherever they are in the world, even in areas nobody has loaded.

    • Channels: 1 to 20, new pads start on channel 1. Tinker goes to the next channel, crouch + Tinker to the previous one.
    • Teleport: press Use on a pad to jump to the next pad on its channel. Two pads on a channel work as a pair. With three or more, each jump goes on to the next pad in a fixed order, so you cycle through them all.
    • No other pad on the channel: "No other Teleport Pad on channel N".
    • What teleports: only the player who used the pad. Vehicles, creations and items stay behind.
    • Cooldown: none. You can jump again as soon as you arrive.

    Power¶

    Each jump costs 1 charge:

    1. From an Inverter wired into the pad, if it has charge (see Power). A pad takes up to 2 wires, and only from Inverters.
    2. Otherwise 1 Battery from your inventory.
    3. With neither: "Teleporting needs 1 charge from wired power, or a battery", and you stay put.

    In a world with unlimited inventory, jumps without wired power are free.

    Warning

    If a pad was picked up or destroyed while nobody was near it, the other pads still think it's there. Jumping to it lands you where it used to be, and after a few seconds you get "The Teleport Pad here is gone - link removed". It is then dropped from the channel.

    Tip

    Wire the pad at your main base to an Inverter on a solar-charged Mega Battery Bank and the jumps out cost nothing from your pockets. A pad at a far outpost with no power still works: that jump takes a Battery.
