    Scrapper¶

    The Scrapper breaks crafted parts back down into 80% of the materials they were made from. Put items in, and a few seconds later the materials are sent down a vacuum pipe into a chest. Which materials come back is random, but you never get more of any one than the recipe used. Items it can't break down pass straight through untouched, so it never jams.

    At a glance

    • Returns 80% of a recipe's materials, rounded down (always at least 1).
    • 3 seconds per input stack, 5 input slots.
    • Needs a chest connected by a vacuum pipe. With no piped chest it won't start.
    • Items with no recipe pass through to the chest unchanged.
    • How to get it: a Traveling Trader blueprint (Uncommon, 72 scrip, Customer reputation), then craft it at the Craftbot.

    Getting a Scrapper¶

    The Scrapper's recipe is a blueprint sold by the Traveling Trader. Buy it once and the whole party can craft Scrappers.

    Blueprint Rarity Price Needs
    Scrapper Uncommon 72 Frontier Scrip Customer reputation (60 scrip spent with him)
    Crafted at Ingredients Time
    Craftbot 30 × Metal Block, 5 × Component Kit, 2 × Circuit Board 20 s

    The Scrapper is 5 × 3 × 4 blocks.

    Setting it up¶

    1. Place the Scrapper.
    2. Connect a chest to it with vacuum pipes. Any of the Scrapper's three pipe rings works. You can pipe in more than one chest.
    3. Press Use on the Scrapper to open its input and drop items in.

    Look at the Scrapper to see what it's doing:

    Status Meaning
    idle Nothing to scrap
    scrapping... N s Working on a stack
    connect a chest with a vacuum pipe No piped chest: it won't start
    piped chest full Every piped chest is full. It waits and tries again each second. Nothing is lost

    It works through one stack at a time and scraps the whole stack in one go. You'll see the materials fly down the pipe into the chest.

    What you get back¶

    For each item, the Scrapper looks up its recipe and adds up all the ingredients. You get 80% of that total, rounded down, and always at least 1. Those units are then drawn at random from the recipe's ingredients, like cards from a deck, so you never get more of one ingredient than the recipe took.

    Example Recipe uses You get back
    1 Scrapper 37 (30 Metal Block, 5 Component Kit, 2 Circuit Board) 29 of those, mixed at random
    1 Pipe Coupler 25 (20 Metal Block, 2 Circuit Board, 2 Glue, 1 Component Kit) 20, mixed at random
    1 Scythe 4 (2 Wood Block, 2 Metal Block) 3, mixed at random

    If an item can be crafted in more than one place, the cheapest recipe counts, so scrapping never pays more than crafting cost.

    Recipes that make several items¶

    Some recipes make several items at once (Carbon Pipes come 10 at a time, for example). These only scrap as whole batches: you need the full amount in one stack. Any leftover too small for a batch passes through untouched.

    You put in Recipe Result
    10 Carbon Pipes 3 Metal Block + 1 Component Kit makes 10 3 units back (80% of 4, rounded down)
    15 Carbon Pipes the same 1 batch scrapped (3 units), 5 pipes come out unchanged
    7 Carbon Pipes the same not a whole batch: all 7 come out unchanged

    What it won't break down¶

    The Scrapper only uses Craftbot and Workbench recipes. Anything else goes through to the chest untouched:

    • items with no recipe, such as raw materials you've harvested, loot-only items and Backpack Expansions
    • food and dishes cooked at the Cookbot or Campfire
    • outfits from the Dressbot
    • refined materials from the Refinery
    • items whose only recipe is at a trader

    Older Scrappers

    Scrappers placed before the pipe update have a second, old output bin. If there's anything in it, it's emptied into the piped chest before anything new is scrapped.

    Craftbot cancel buttons¶

    While a Craftbot window is open, a strip of red CANCEL buttons sits between the window and your hotbar, one under each crafting slot that's in use.

    • Cancel takes that craft off the queue and gives all its ingredients back to the inventory of whoever clicked.
    • If your inventory can't hold them, you get an inventory full message and the craft stays queued.
    • A repeating craft that's only waiting for materials has nothing to refund. Cancelling it just takes it off the queue and stops it pulling from pipes.
    • Finished crafts have no button. Collect them as usual.
    • Empty and locked slots have no button either.

    The strip fits 16:9, 16:10, 21:9 and 4:3 screens. It replaced the old crouch + Use Craft Queue window (from 1.3.0, crouch + Use on a Craftbot no longer opens it).
