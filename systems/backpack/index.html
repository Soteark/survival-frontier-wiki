    Backpack Expansions¶

    A Backpack Expansion permanently adds one row of 10 slots to your inventory. Each player can use up to 10, for 100 extra slots in all. Backpack Expansions can't be crafted. You find them in loot far from the crash site, pull them up in Sunken Crates while fishing far out, win them from top-tier contracts, or buy them from the Traveling Trader once he trusts you.

    At a glance

    • +1 row (10 slots) per expansion, up to +10 rows per player.
    • Per player: the extra rows are yours alone, saved with the world, and still there when you rejoin.
    • To use: hold it and keep Create (left-click) held for 5 seconds.
    • Where to get one: ruin chests and epic or legendary loot crates far from the crash site, far-out Sunken Crates, tier 5 contracts, and the Traveling Trader (800 scrip, Partner reputation).
    • No recipe. They stack up to 10.

    How to use one¶

    1. Put the Backpack Expansion in your hotbar and select it.
    2. Hold Create (left-click). A progress circle fills over 5 seconds.
    3. When it's full, one expansion is used up and your inventory grows by one row. A message shows your new total, for example "Backpack expanded! +3 rows (3/10)".

    Let go before the circle fills and nothing happens, so you can't use one by accident.

    Once you have all 10 rows, you get "Your backpack is already at the maximum (+10 rows)" and the expansion isn't used up. Give the spare to a friend.

    Per player, and permanent¶

    • The rows belong to the player who used the expansion, not to the world or the party.
    • They're saved with the world. Leave and rejoin and your inventory is still the bigger size.
    • In someone else's world, your rows are saved in that world. They don't follow you to other saves.
    Expansions used Extra slots
    1 +10
    5 +50
    10 (maximum) +100

    Where to find them¶

    Loot far from the crash site¶

    Backpack Expansions can turn up in ruin chests and in epic and legendary loot crates, but only far from the start:

    Distance from the crash site Chance
    Under 600 m none
    600 m to 2,500 m rises steadily with distance
    2,500 m and more full chance

    At full chance, that's about:

    Container Chance of a Backpack Expansion
    Ruin chest about 1%
    Epic loot crate about 3%
    Legendary loot crate about 10%

    It's the farthest player who counts

    The game can't tell where a chest is, so it goes by the farthest player's distance from the crash site at the moment the chest fills. Containers fill as areas load around players, so explore far out yourself to get the full chance.

    Sunken Crates (fishing)¶

    When fishing, some casts bring up a Sunken Crate instead of a fish. In the far band (2,500 m and more from the crash site) a Sunken Crate also has a 5% chance to hold a Backpack Expansion, on top of its usual loot. Crates from the near and mid bands never have one.

    Contracts¶

    Only tier 5 contracts can pay a Backpack Expansion. The chance is rolled once, when the contract is done, so you can't see it on the board beforehand:

    Contract Chance at tier 5
    Supply Run none
    Courier 3%
    Pest Control 8%
    Survey Post 8%
    Catch Order 8%
    Salvage 15%

    When you're lucky, the completion message says "- with a Backpack Expansion!", and it's part of the reward you claim at the board.

    Traveling Trader¶

    The Traveling Trader sells Backpack Expansions on his Gear tab:

    Price Needs
    800 Frontier Scrip Partner reputation (the party has spent 600 scrip with him in total)

    The expansion stays on show, locked, until you reach Partner.

    Tip

    Expansions are items, so whoever finds or buys one can hand it to anyone in the party.
