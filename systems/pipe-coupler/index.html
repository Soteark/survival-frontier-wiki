    Pipe Coupler¶

    The Pipe Coupler joins two vacuum pipe networks that aren't built together, such as a lift platform and your base, or a supply truck and your factory. Pipe a coupler into each network and push their flat faces together. While they're docked, the two networks work as one: a Craftbot on one side uses the chests on the other side, pumps fill them, and items travel both ways. Pull the two sides apart and the networks are separate again.

    At a glance

    • Size: 3 × 3 × 2 blocks. The pipe goes in at the back, and the flat face at the front is the docking face.
    • Docks when two couplers' flat faces touch, face to face. The lamps light up while docked.
    • Works both ways, and through chains of several docks.
    • Each side uses its own chests first, then the ones across the dock.
    • How to get it: a Traveling Trader blueprint (Common, 30 scrip, no reputation needed), then craft it at the Craftbot.

    Getting a Pipe Coupler¶

    The recipe is a blueprint from the Traveling Trader. Buy it once and the whole party can craft couplers.

    Blueprint Rarity Price Needs
    Pipe Coupler Common 30 Frontier Scrip nothing (Stranger reputation)
    Crafted at Ingredients Time
    Craftbot 20 × Metal Block, 2 × Circuit Board, 2 × Glue, 1 × Component Kit 15 s

    Couplers stack up to 10.

    How docking works¶

    1. On each network, build a coupler and connect a vacuum pipe to the opening at its back.
    2. Bring the two couplers together so their flat front faces touch, facing each other.
    3. When they're docked, both couplers' lamps light up. Look at one to see docked: pipes joined (or not docked).
    Rule Value
    Faces must be within 0.2 m of each other (a few centimetres off still docks)
    Faces must point at each other (roughly face to face, not side by side or the same way round)
    Docking is noticed within a quarter of a second
    Undocking as soon as the faces move apart, or one coupler is removed

    You can't build anything onto the flat docking face. It's kept clear so it can touch the other coupler.

    Line them up

    Moving one coupler a whole block out of line is enough to undock it. Guide rails or a stop block on your lift help it land in the same spot every time.

    What joins¶

    While docked, anything on one network that uses vacuum pipes sees the containers on the other network too:

    • A Craftbot on one side lists and spends from the chests on the other side, and can put finished items into them.
    • Pumps on either side can fill the chests on the other side.
    • Item animations follow the pipes on both sides of the dock, so you can see items travelling through the couplers.

    The join works both ways, and it chains. If network A docks with B, and B docks with C somewhere else, a Craftbot on A can use the chests on C.

    Situation What happens
    The item is in a chest on your own side Your own chests are used first
    Your own chests are full Items overflow into the chests across the dock
    A chest is reachable two ways It's only counted once
    The item isn't anywhere on either side Nothing is spent, and the craft isn't queued

    Note

    Couplers only join pipe networks. Wires, power and logic aren't carried across a dock.

    Use cases¶

    • Lift platforms. Keep a chest on a lift platform and dock it against your base's pipes at the bottom. Your base Craftbots can use what's on the lift.
    • Supply vehicles. Put a coupler on the back of a truck that carries chests. Back it up to a coupler on your base and the factory can pull from the truck.
    • Separate buildings. Join two pipe networks on separate creations without welding them together.

    Limits¶

    • The two couplers must be docked face to face. Couplers side by side or back to back don't join anything.
    • Each coupler follows up to 4,096 pipe pieces of its own network.
    • If you build or remove pipes on a docked network, the coupler picks up the change within about 5 seconds. Docking or undocking updates it straight away.
    • The Workbench has no pipes, so couplers make no difference to it.
