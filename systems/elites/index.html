    Elite Creatures¶

    Far from the crash site, a few of the robots you meet are elites: tougher versions of the usual Totebots, Tapebots, Haybots and Farmbots, painted dark red, with brighter eyes and sparks crackling around them. They hit just as hard as normal robots but take three times as many hits to bring down, and they drop much better loot.

    At a glance

    • Off until you ask for them: elites start off in every world. Once the party reaches Regular reputation with the Traveling Trader, anyone can turn them on from his Talk tab, and turn them off again there.
    • Where: wild robots far from the crash site. None within 600 m, up to 1 in 20 robots from 2,500 m out.
    • Tougher: they take 3x as many hits as a normal robot of the same kind, from everything: guns, hammers, explosions and ramming. Their attacks do normal damage.
    • Look: dark red paint, glowing eyes and sparks.
    • Loot: the robot's normal loot twice, plus one pick from a special elite table, and sometimes a Backpack Expansion.
    • Pest Control: tier 4 and 5 hunt packs sometimes include one elite. Destroying it pays 25% more scrip for that contract.
    • Frontier Setup: the host can turn Elite Creatures off when starting a new world; then the trader never offers them. It needs the Traveling Trader feature.

    Turning them on and off¶

    Elites are off in every world, including worlds from before they existed, until someone asks the Traveling Trader for them:

    1. Reach Regular reputation with him (the party has spent 250 scrip at his Stall in total). Before that, his Talk tab only says "Spend more with me and I might share what I hear out there (Regular)".
    2. Open his Stall, go to the Talk tab and read what he has heard: "Word from the wastes: the bots out past the ridges have started calling for backup. Bigger, meaner, painted red. Want me to spread the word that you are looking for a fight?"
    3. Press Bring it on. Elites are now on for the whole world, and everyone gets a message naming who did it, for example "Ann stirred up the robots. Elite robots now roam the far wilds." Not now leaves them off.

    Changed your mind? The same tab then offers Call off the backup. That stops new elites from appearing (everyone is told who called it off); elites already out there stay until they're destroyed. You can turn them on again at any time.

    Any player in the party can turn elites on or call them off. It's saved with the world.

    Where they appear¶

    Once elites are on, any wild Totebot, Tapebot, Haybot or Farmbot can be an elite. Whether a robot is one is decided once, when it first appears in the world, and depends on how far it is from the crash site:

    Distance from the crash site Chance a new robot is an elite
    Under 600 m none
    1,000 m about 1 in 100
    1,550 m about 1 in 40
    2,500 m and more 1 in 20

    Robots that are already in the world stay as they are: an elite is still an elite when you come back, and a normal robot never becomes one. Raiders attacking your farm, robots in the underground areas and Leaf Totebots (the ones disguised as bushes) are never elites. If you paint an elite, it loses its glow and sparks but stays an elite.

    What they drop¶

    An elite drops:

    1. its normal loot twice (the same kind of loot a normal robot of its kind drops),
    2. one pick from its elite table below,
    3. and sometimes a Backpack Expansion (see below).

    An elite that drowns (dies in water) drops only its normal loot once, like any robot.

    Item Chance
    2 Component Kits 30%
    3 Prime Bait 25%
    Rare Outfit Package 23%
    2 Batteries 20%
    Frontier Rod 2%
    Item Chance
    Rare Outfit Package 31%
    3 Component Kits 25%
    5 Prime Bait 20%
    3 Batteries 20%
    Frontier Rod 4%
    Item Chance
    Epic Outfit Package 45%
    Rare Outfit Package 20%
    5 Component Kits 15%
    Frontier Rod 10%
    8 Prime Bait 10%

    In worlds with Fishing turned off, elites never drop Prime Bait or the Frontier Rod; the other items share the chance.

    Backpack Expansions¶

    An elite can also drop a Backpack Expansion, more often the farther out it was:

    Elite Chance at 2,500 m and more
    Totebot or Tapebot 2%
    Haybot 4%
    Farmbot 10%

    The chance is zero within 600 m and rises steadily up to 2,500 m, like Backpack Expansions in chests. If the player nearest to the elite already has all 10 backpack rows, it drops another pick from its elite table instead. Worlds with Backpack Expansions turned off never get them from elites.

    Elites in Pest Control¶

    While elites are on, at tier 4 and 5 a Pest Control pack has a 35% chance to include one elite robot of the kind the contract asks for. It:

    • takes 3x the hits and has the elite look,
    • counts as one kill for the hunt like the rest of the pack,
    • drops the pack's normal (smaller) loot once, plus one pick from its elite table and the same Backpack Expansion chance as a wild elite,
    • adds 25% to the contract's scrip reward when you destroy it. An elite that drowns doesn't pay the bonus.

    There's at most one elite per contract. If the pack has to be replaced later, the new robots don't include another one.
