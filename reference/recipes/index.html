    Crafting Recipes¶

    Every recipe Survival Frontier adds, by crafting station. Recipes marked Trader are locked until the party buys the blueprint from the Traveling Trader; everything else is available from the start of a new world. Times are the station's craft time per batch.

    Cooking

    Cookbot and Campfire dishes are listed with their tiers and buffs on the Dishes page. Most dishes also need researching first - see the Frontier Kitchen.
