    All Items¶

    Every item Survival Frontier adds, grouped by system, with where it comes from and what it's used for. For full recipe tables see Crafting Recipes; dishes and fish have their own Dishes and Fish pages.

    Jump to: Remote Control · Gadgets · Power · Frontier Kitchen · Fishing · Land Clearing · Contracts · Logistics · Gyro & Signal Parts (experimental) · Building Blocks

    Remote Control¶

    Remote¶

    Handheld controller for Remote Controller blocks.
    Aim at a Remote Controller and press Create to take control. Drive with WASD, hotbar keys work like a seat. Press Interact or put the Remote away to let go.

    Get it: Crafted at the Craftbot; recipe from the Traveling Trader (Rare blueprint Remote Control, 144 scrip, needs Regular reputation).
    Guide: Remote Control

    Remote Controller¶

    Level 1. Wire it like a driver's seat: engines, steering bearings and hotbar parts. Drive the creation from outside with the Remote.
    Aim the Remote at it and press Create to take control.

    Get it: Crafted at the Craftbot; recipe from the Traveling Trader (Rare blueprint Remote Control, 144 scrip, needs Regular reputation).
    Stack size: 5
    Guide: Remote Control

    Gadgets¶

    Backpack Expansion¶

    Permanently adds one row (10 slots) to your inventory. Hold it and keep Create held for 5 seconds to use - let go early to cancel.
    Up to 10 extra rows per player. Rare - found far from home or bought from traders.

    Get it: Found in far-off ruin loot and Sunken Crates, from top-rank contracts, or bought from the Traveling Trader (800 scrip, Partner reputation).
    Stack size: 10
    Guide: Gadgets

    Mega Battery¶

    Holds as much power as 100 batteries. Only fits in a Mega Battery Bank, and is used up when drained.

    Get it: Crafted at the Craftbot; recipe from the Traveling Trader (Rare blueprint Mega Battery, 144 scrip, needs Regular reputation).
    Stack size: 10
    Guide: Gadgets

    Mega Battery Bank¶

    Holds one Mega Battery (100 charge) or Rechargeable Battery (25). The gauge ticks down as it's used; the slot unlocks for a fresh battery when it's empty.
    Wire FROM: a Charge Controller (to recharge it from solar), or nothing Wire INTO: electric engines, drills, growbeds - or an Inverter to run Floodlights, Chargers and Teleport Pads
    Full solar setup: Solar Panels > Charge Controller > Mega Battery Bank > Inverter > Floodlight / Charger / Teleport Pad

    Get it: Crafted at the Craftbot; recipe from the Traveling Trader (Legendary blueprint Mega Battery Bank, 300 scrip, needs Partner reputation).
    Stack size: 10
    Guide: Gadgets

    Remote Camera¶

    A camera for remote-controlled creations. Wire it into a Remote Controller (camera first, then the controller).
    While piloting with the Remote, press Jump to cycle through the controller's cameras and back to your own view.

    Get it: Crafted at the Craftbot; recipe from the Traveling Trader (Rare blueprint Remote Camera, 144 scrip, needs Regular reputation).
    Stack size: 10
    Guide: Gadgets

    Scrapper¶

    Breaks crafted parts back down into 80% of their crafting materials - which materials you get is random, but never more of one than the recipe used.
    Needs a chest connected with a vacuum pipe (any of its three pipe rings): the materials are sent down the pipe into it. Use: open the input. Items with no recipe, and leftovers too few for a whole recipe batch, come out untouched.

    Get it: Crafted at the Craftbot; recipe from the Traveling Trader (Uncommon blueprint Scrapper, 72 scrip, needs Customer reputation).
    Stack size: 1
    Guide: Gadgets

    Teleport Pad¶

    Links to every Teleport Pad on the same channel, anywhere in the world. Interact to jump to the next pad. Each jump costs 1 charge from a wired Inverter, or else 1 battery from your inventory. Tinker: next channel (crouch for previous).
    Wire FROM: an Inverter (optional, to pay with stored power) Wire INTO: nothing

    Get it: Crafted at the Craftbot; recipe from the Traveling Trader (Legendary blueprint Teleport Pad, 300 scrip, needs Partner reputation).
    Stack size: 2
    Guide: Gadgets

    Wireless Receiver¶

    Turns on while any Wireless Transmitter on the same channel is on. Wire it to engines, doors, lights, logic - anything a switch can drive.
    Interact: next channel. Tinker: previous channel.

    Get it: Crafted at the Craftbot; recipe from the Traveling Trader (Uncommon blueprint Wireless Link, 72 scrip, needs Customer reputation).
    Stack size: 10
    Guide: Gadgets

    Wireless Transmitter¶

    Broadcasts on/off on a channel. It is on while any button, switch, logic gate or seat hotbar slot wired into it is on.
    Interact: next channel. Tinker: previous channel. Works anywhere in the loaded world.

    Get it: Crafted at the Craftbot; recipe from the Traveling Trader (Uncommon blueprint Wireless Link, 72 scrip, needs Customer reputation).
    Stack size: 10
    Guide: Gadgets

    Power¶

    Charge Controller¶

    Level 1. Collects power from Solar Panels and safely charges a Mega Battery Bank. Handles up to 2 panels. Tinker to upgrade for more panels.
    Wire FROM: Solar Panels Wire INTO: a Mega Battery Bank
    Full solar setup: Solar Panels > Charge Controller > Mega Battery Bank > Inverter > Floodlight / Charger / Teleport Pad

    Get it: Crafted at the Craftbot; recipe from the Traveling Trader (Uncommon blueprint Charge Controller, 72 scrip, needs Customer reputation).
    Stack size: 10
    Guide: Power

    Charger¶

    Recharges Empty Rechargeable Batteries to 25 charge. Holds 4.
    Wire FROM: an Inverter Wire INTO: nothing
    Full solar setup: Solar Panels > Charge Controller > Mega Battery Bank > Inverter > Floodlight / Charger / Teleport Pad

    Get it: Crafted at the Craftbot; recipe from the Traveling Trader (Uncommon blueprint Charger, 72 scrip, needs Customer reputation).
    Stack size: 10
    Guide: Power

    Floodlight¶

    Bright light for outposts. Shines while a switch or Light Sensor wired into it is on. Uses 1 charge per 12 hours of light.
    Wire FROM: an Inverter (power) + a Light Sensor, switch or button (on/off) Wire INTO: nothing
    Full solar setup: Solar Panels > Charge Controller > Mega Battery Bank > Inverter > Floodlight / Charger / Teleport Pad

    Get it: Crafted at the Craftbot; recipe from the Traveling Trader (Common blueprint Floodlight, 30 scrip, needs Stranger reputation).
    Stack size: 10
    Guide: Power

    Inverter¶

    Level 1. Turns stored Bank power into the power that Floodlights, Chargers and Teleport Pads run on. 80% efficient, runs up to 2 devices. Tinker to upgrade. Electric engines, drills and growbeds don't need one - wire them straight from the Bank.
    Wire FROM: a Mega Battery Bank Wire INTO: Floodlights, Chargers, Teleport Pads
    Full solar setup: Solar Panels > Charge Controller > Mega Battery Bank > Inverter > Floodlight / Charger / Teleport Pad

    Get it: Crafted at the Craftbot; recipe from the Traveling Trader (Uncommon blueprint Inverter, 72 scrip, needs Customer reputation).
    Stack size: 10
    Guide: Power

    Light Sensor¶

    Turns on when it gets dark. Interact to flip it to 'on when light'.
    Wire FROM: nothing Wire INTO: a Floodlight - or anything a switch can control

    Get it: Crafted at the Craftbot; recipe from the Traveling Trader (Common blueprint Light Sensor, 30 scrip, needs Stranger reputation).
    Stack size: 10
    Guide: Power

    Rechargeable Battery¶

    Holds 25 charge. Put it in a Mega Battery Bank. When drained it comes back Empty - recharge it in a Charger.

    Stack size: 10
    Guide: Power

    Rechargeable Battery (Empty)¶

    Put it in a Charger (powered by an Inverter) to recharge it to 25.

    Get it: Crafted at the Craftbot; recipe from the Traveling Trader (Common blueprint Rechargeable Battery, 30 scrip, needs Stranger reputation).
    Stack size: 10
    Guide: Power

    Solar Panel¶

    Makes power from sunlight: about 15 charge per sunny day. Nothing at night, less in clouds, very little in rain, and nothing if anything is above it.
    Wire FROM: nothing Wire INTO: a Charge Controller
    Full solar setup: Solar Panels > Charge Controller > Mega Battery Bank > Inverter > Floodlight / Charger / Teleport Pad

    Get it: Crafted at the Craftbot; recipe from the Traveling Trader (Uncommon blueprint Solar Panel, 72 scrip, needs Customer reputation).
    Stack size: 10
    Guide: Power

    Frontier Kitchen¶

    Baked Potato¶

    Classics cooking. Three potatoes, one hot panel, no notes.
    Restores 48 health. Regenerates 1 health per second for 15 s. Farmed crops for one craft: raid level 1.
    Always known. Every Classics dish you cook earns 1 Classics mastery for researching Campfire dishes.

    Get it: Crafted at the Cookbot or Campfire.
    Restores: 48 HP
    Stack size: 10
    Guide: Frontier Kitchen

    Banana Bread¶

    Homestead cooking (tier 2). Dense, sweet and travels well.
    Restores 80 health. Regenerates 2 health per second for 40 s. Take 15% less damage for 3 min. Farmed crops for one craft: raid level 1.
    Food buffs don't stack - eating again keeps whichever is stronger, or longer if equal.
    Research it at a Cookbot for 5 Campfire mastery. Every Campfire dish you cook earns 1.

    Get it: Crafted at the Cookbot.
    Restores: 80 HP

    Guide: Frontier Kitchen

    Set Point¶

    Outputs a fixed number you set, -100 to 100. Use as the base throttle a hover needs. Use/Tinker = +/-1, crouch for steps of 10.
    Wire FROM: nothing Wire INTO: signal parts
    Stabilizer parts: Gyro Flywheel > Angle Encoder > Gain / Rate / Mixer / Flip / Clamp / Set Point > Throttle Thruster. Scope shows any signal.

    Get it: Crafted at the Craftbot; recipe from the Traveling Trader (Uncommon blueprint Signal Parts, 72 scrip, needs Customer reputation).
    Stack size: 20
    Guide: Gyro & Signal Parts

    Throttle Thruster¶

    Pushes with exactly its signal as a percent of full thrust (0-100). Full thrust holds up about 800 kg. Wire in a switch to arm it: with any switch wired in, one must be on. Uses about 1 charge per 30 s at full thrust.
    Wire FROM: an Inverter (power), signal parts, switches Wire INTO: nothing
    Stabilizer parts: Gyro Flywheel > Angle Encoder > Gain / Rate / Mixer / Flip / Clamp / Set Point > Throttle Thruster. Scope shows any signal.

    Get it: Crafted at the Craftbot; recipe from the Traveling Trader (Rare blueprint Throttle Thruster, 144 scrip, needs Regular reputation).
    Stack size: 5
    Guide: Gyro & Signal Parts
