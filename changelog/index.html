    Changelog¶

    1.4.0¶

    • New: the Traveling Trader. He camps 300-1500 m from the Hideout and moves on every 2-3 days. Follow the map marker or type /trader. His Stall sells the Frontier machine blueprints (unlocked for the whole party), Backpack Expansions and 11 new trader-only dish recipes, all for Frontier Scrip. Spending scrip with him raises your reputation (Stranger, Customer, Regular, Partner), which opens the rarer stock. New worlds no longer start with the machine blueprints; existing worlds keep the ones they have.
    • New: the Pipe Coupler (trader blueprint). Two couplers dock when their flat faces touch, for example a lift platform set down against your base, and join the two vacuum pipe networks, so a Craftbot can use chests on the other side.
    • New: Outpost Boards (trader, Standing rank 3). Put one out in the world, 1.5 km from any other board, and new location contracts get posted around it as well as around the Hideout.
    • Contracts: the Contract Board is now the one at the Hideout (the craftable board is retired). A "New postings" button replaces every unaccepted offer for scrip; accepting no longer posts a free new offer. Abandoning puts the contract back and refunds anything handed in. Pest Control hunts are now at marked sites, Survey Posts need a real built structure (powered at higher tiers), every active location contract has a marker, and sites no longer land in lakes. Rewards and Standing ranks rebalanced; older saves keep their rank.
    • Fishing: a compact reeling panel just above the hotbar replaces the big bars over the crosshair, running fish splash instead of scraping, and in first person the line starts at the rod tip. The Carbon Rod is now a trader blueprint in new worlds.
    • Food: the Cookbot has a "Cookable now" tab that counts chests piped into it. Expedition and Feast dishes heal properly under the 100 HP cap, and recipes were rebalanced (fewer raid crops, Fish Chowder reworked).
    • Fixed: Floodlights now light up. Shots pass through bushes and plants again.

    1.3.0¶

    • New: the Scythe and land clearing. Craft a Scythe at the Craftbot (2 Wood + 2 Metal) and clear bushes, shrubs, saplings, ferns, reeds, burnt bushes, fallen logs and stumps, leaf and hay piles, fluff plants and coral. It cuts the plant you're aiming at and everything in a short arc in front of you. Cut plants don't grow back, and the Scythe never hurts creatures, players, builds or trees. This works in existing worlds too: plants become clearable the first time you go near them.
    • New: the Fishing Rod can also be crafted at the Craftbot, not just the Workbench.
    • Changed: crouch + Use on a Craftbot no longer opens the old Craft Queue window. Cancel crafts with the Cancel buttons under the Craftbot window.

    1.2.1¶

    • New: /bugreport also saves the reports you send to BugReports.json in your Survival Frontier folder, newest first, so you don't have to dig through the game log. This works whether you're hosting or joined someone else's game.

    1.1.1¶

    • Fixed: players joining a game could see the wrong version, missing items (Campfire, Contract Board, Frontier dishes), recipes that wouldn't craft, or raw names like obj_frontier_food_... after an update.
    • New: version check. When you join, chat shows your Survival Frontier version and the host's, and warns if they differ. Type /frontierversion to check any time.
    • Fixed: a HUD error with the food buff icons.
